Two survivors. A dead city. No way out.
Driving Dead is a never-ending story — a couple holed up in a self-built solar camper van on a carpark rooftop in a zombie-overrun city, with no script, no crew, and no rescue. The story unfolds four times a day. The characters change, harden, and surprise you as time goes on — no one controls them. Each night the Oracle takes its turn — the dead, a survivor, a system failure — that can shift the whole storyline. This entire world runs at zero cost.
